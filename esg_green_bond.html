ESG / Green Bond Verification

The green bond market is the fastest-growing segment of fixed income — and its most trust-deficient. Issuers label bonds "green" with minimal scrutiny. AI verification addresses both dimensions of the problem: whether the bond's declared use-of-proceeds actually aligns with recognised taxonomies, and whether the issuer is deploying the capital as promised after issuance.

NLP / Taxonomy Matching Alternative Data Monitoring Satellite Imagery Analysis Post-Issuance Tracking ★ Hexaware Proposed
The Gap
Greenwashing — issuers labelling bonds "green" without meaningful underlying project alignment — is structurally unchecked. Manual verification of use-of-proceeds documents at scale is impossible, and no system tracks post-issuance capital deployment against original commitments.
AI Solution
NLP verifies alignment of declared projects against the EU Green Bond Standard, ICMA principles, and SEBI framework at issuance. Alternative data then monitors actual capital deployment post-issuance — satellite imagery, contractor records, and environmental filings confirming the project is real and progressing.
Value Created
A defensible, independently verified ESG score for every green bond — enabling genuine sustainable investing rather than label-dependent allocation that exposes investors to regulatory and reputational risk from greenwashing.
Strategic Framing — Trust in the Green Market
"A 'green' label without verification is not ESG investing — it is ESG theatre. The only way to build genuine trust in the green bond market is to move from self-attestation to independent, machine-verified alignment — checking not just what the issuer promised, but what they actually did with the money."
⚖️
Live Regulatory Mandates — This Is Not Aspirational
Green bond verification is a compliance obligation in 2026, not a nice-to-have. Regulated funds must demonstrate ESG alignment under SFDR (EU), MiFID II sustainability preferences, and SEBI's Green Bond Disclosure Framework. Non-compliant classification exposes fund managers to regulatory sanction and investor litigation.

Dimension 1 · At Issuance
📋
Use-of-Proceeds Verification
NLP reads the full use-of-proceeds framework and project descriptions at bond issuance — matching declared projects against three taxonomy definitions simultaneously to produce a multi-framework alignment score.
Document ingestion — full use-of-proceeds prospectus section parsed, project categories extracted and classified
Three-framework matching — EU Green Bond Standard, ICMA Green Bond Principles, and SEBI framework applied simultaneously; divergences flagged explicitly
Proceeds segregation check — XBRL data confirms dedicated account structure exists before labelling can be accepted
Alignment score — 0–100 score with framework-specific breakdown and a plain-language flag list for the analyst
Dimension 2 · Post-Issuance
🛰️
Capital Deployment Monitoring
Continuous tracking of whether the issuer is actually deploying capital as promised — using alternative data to verify project reality on the ground, not just on paper. A bond issued to fund solar infrastructure should show actual solar infrastructure being built.
Satellite imagery — project site activity monitored via satellite; construction progress compared against declared timeline and capital disbursement schedule
Contractor and permit filings — publicly available construction permits, environmental clearances, and contractor registrations cross-referenced against project location and scope
Impact report monitoring — annual impact report publication dates tracked; overdue reports flagged immediately as a governance concern
Deployment score decay — if no verifiable project activity within 12 months of issuance, the bond's alignment score is automatically downgraded and the investor alerted

Research Notes

The Scale of the Problem
Market sizeThe global green bond market exceeded $2 trillion in cumulative issuance by 2026. India's green bond market alone is projected to reach ₹5 lakh crore by 2030 under SEBI's green finance framework.
Greenwashing evidenceA 2024 analysis by Carbon Tracker found that 23% of self-labelled green bonds in emerging markets could not demonstrate measurable environmental outcomes two years post-issuance — a systemic failure of the self-attestation model.
Regulatory directionThe EU Green Bond Standard (effective December 2024) introduced mandatory external review requirements and post-issuance reporting obligations — creating a compliance driver that makes AI verification economically necessary at scale.
The Dimension 2 gapNo current product systematically monitors post-issuance capital deployment using alternative data at scale. This is the genuine white space — issuance verification tools exist (Clarity AI, MSCI), post-issuance monitoring does not.
Technical Architecture & Competitors
NLP modelDomain-specific NLP fine-tuned on green bond frameworks — must understand that "climate-aligned infrastructure" means different things under EU taxonomy vs ICMA principles vs SEBI, and classify the same project description accordingly.
Satellite dataCommercial satellite providers (Planet Labs, Maxar) provide sub-weekly imagery of project sites. Change detection algorithms identify construction activity, vegetation changes, and infrastructure installation consistent with declared project type.
Clarity AIThe closest existing competitor — provides ESG data and taxonomy alignment scores at issuance. Does not perform post-issuance capital deployment monitoring. The proposed Dimension 2 goes meaningfully beyond what Clarity AI offers.
MSCI ESGBroad ESG screening tools; green bond specific scoring is a subset of a larger data product. Less granular than a dedicated green bond verification system and does not address the post-issuance monitoring gap.
