What HNIs Currently Face

Four Structural Gaps
💧
Invisible Liquidity Risk
No retail platform shows HNIs how quickly they can exit a bond position in a stressed market. They discover illiquidity at the worst possible moment.
📰
Lagging Credit Ratings
S&P and Moody's downgrades arrive weeks after the market has already moved. By the time an HNI reads the downgrade, the loss has already happened.
📈
No Duration Management
HNIs choose bonds based on headline yield without understanding that a +50bps rate rise can erase months of coupon income through principal loss.
🔲
Black-Box Risk Scores
When risk scores exist at all, they are opaque numbers from agency models that cannot be questioned, understood, or cross-referenced by the investor.

The Product Strategy
ArchitectureProduct layer built on three independently validated institutional engines — not a new invention but a new integration
Target segmentHNIs with ₹50L+ investable corpus, existing bond market participants seeking better tools than brokers provide
DifferentiatorTransparency — BondPulse explains its scores. Every other platform gives a number. BondPulse gives a number and a chart and a reason
Lot size constraintBond minimum denominations (₹10–50L) limit this to HNIs — a smaller addressable market but a far more credible product promise and a self-selecting high-trust audience
The Engine/Car Distinction
The enginesLiquidity Risk Scoring, Yield Curve Modelling, and Creditworthiness Assessment are institutional backend tools — internal-facing, technically complex, regulatory-grade
BondPulseThe consumer product that packages these engines for HNIs — user-facing, plain-language, actionable. The engines are the infrastructure; BondPulse is the interface
The simulatorRepurposes the OTC Market Simulator — a backend bank training tool — as a retail trust-building feature. Try-Before-You-Buy converts a boring educational step into a sales engagement moment
